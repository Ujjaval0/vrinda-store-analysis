## 📌 Project Overview

Vrinda Store is a mid-scale Indian apparel retailer selling across 7 major e-commerce platforms. This project skips the opinions and goes straight to the data — **32,047 real orders, ₹21.18 Crore in annual revenue, and 10+ states** analyzed across multiple customer segments.

What we found challenges almost everything management assumed about channels, customers, and growth.

The final output is a **fully responsive, editorial-style data report** built with HTML, CSS, and Chart.js — inspired by modern data journalism.

## 🔍 Dashboards & Analysis

### Dashboard 1 — Executive Summary
- 64% of all revenue is driven by **female customers**
- Top 3 channels (Amazon, Myntra, Flipkart) account for **80% of revenue** — a concentration risk
- Cancellation rate is below **3%**, far better than industry average

### Dashboard 2 — Financial Health & Revenue Split
- Women's Revenue: **₹13.56 Cr** | Men's Revenue: **₹7.61 Cr**
- Healthy AOV of **₹6,609** — a strong investor benchmark metric
- Gender doughnut chart reveals the core revenue driver clearly

### Dashboard 3 — Customer Segmentation
| Segment | Share | Strategic Label |
|---|---|---|
| Adult Women (30–50) | 34.6% | Cash Cow |
| Teen Girls | 21.1% | Growth Future |
| Adult Men | 15.5% | Immediate Gap |

### Dashboard 4 — Channel Distribution
- Amazon leads with **35.5%** revenue share
- Top 3 platforms = **80%** of total revenue
- Meesho & Nalli combined = **~9%** — huge social-commerce upside

### Dashboard 5 — Geographic Footprint
- **South India** contributes **52.6%** of revenue — clear market leadership
- Maharashtra alone: **₹2.99 Cr** (top state)
- North India (Delhi, Punjab, Haryana) is largely **untapped**

### Dashboard 6 — Seasonality & Timing
- Peak months: **Jan–Apr** (wedding + festival season)
- Lowest months: **Sep–Nov**
- **16% variance** between peak and trough — predictable enough to build a media calendar

### Dashboard 7 — Operational Excellence
- Delivered: **89.3%** | Returns: **3.3%** | Other: ~7.4%
- Reducing returns to 2% via better imagery = **₹40–50 Lakhs saved annually**

## 🎯 Strategic Recommendations

### Pillar 01 — Market Penetration
- Aggressive push into North India (Delhi, Punjab, Haryana)
- Target: **+30% new customers** within 12 months
- Expand state coverage: 10 → 18 states

### Pillar 02 — Product Diversification
- Launch a dedicated **Men's Wear** collection
- Retain 60% marketing budget on Adult Women segment
- Target Teen Girls cohort with trend-driven campaigns

### Pillar 03 — Channel Strategy
- Reduce Amazon dependency from 35% → **below 30%**
- Scale **Meesho** for social-commerce audience
- Launch a **D2C website** for improved margins

### 📈 Financial Target: ₹25 Cr+ in FY25
> By unlocking the male segment, entering North Indian markets, and improving operational efficiency, the model projects a **20%+ revenue increase** in FY25.

## 💡 Key Insights Summary

1. **Women (30–50) are the backbone** — any strategy must retain this segment first
2. **Channel concentration is the biggest risk** — 80% revenue from 3 platforms
3. **North India is a greenfield opportunity** — low penetration, high e-commerce growth

5. **Operations are healthy** — 89.3% delivery rate is an asset to leverage in marketing
